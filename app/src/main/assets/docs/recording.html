Recording

Start reception before recording. Modem raw IQ is recorded at 7.68 MS/s before display-band filtering. The receiver offers 5 MHz raw, 2 MHz /3 FIR and 1 MHz /6 FIR display paths. Time/size limits stop a recording automatically. For file playback use the recorded sample rate, frequency and numeric sample format. Preserve recordings before reinstalling the app or changing its signing certificate.
