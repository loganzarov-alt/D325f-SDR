Audio and demodulation

Select AM, NFM, WFM, USB, LSB or CW and tune the channel inside the captured bandwidth. Channel width controls the demodulation filter. Squelch mutes weak signals. Audio gain and mute affect playback. Keep-channel-centred follows tuning. While audio demodulation is active, IQ continuity is prioritised over discarding old display packets.
